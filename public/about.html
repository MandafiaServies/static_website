Our Story

Over 25 Years of Keeping Facilities at Their Best

Mandafia Services started with a small housekeeping team and a simple belief: well-maintained spaces make people more productive. Today we manage more than 250 sites — corporate offices, manufacturing plants, malls, hospitals, schools and residential societies.

Our integrated approach means one contract, one point of contact and one team accountable for results.

  • ISO 9001, 14001 & 45001 aligned processes
  • PSARA-licensed security operations
  • Statutory compliance (PF, ESI, labour laws) handled end-to-end
  • Dedicated account manager for every client
