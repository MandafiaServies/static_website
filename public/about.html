About Mandafia Services

Cleaner, Safer, Smarter Workplaces: Managed for You.

Who We Are

Mandafia Services is an integrated facility management company based in Gurugram. We bring housekeeping, security, technical maintenance, landscaping, pest control and manpower supply together under one roof. Our clients deal with one accountable partner instead of many separate vendors.

Behind Mandafia are 25+ years of corporate industry experience. Our leadership and teams know the standards that leading multinational companies expect, and we bring that discipline to every site we manage.

Our Story

Mandafia grew out of a simple observation. Most facilities don’t struggle because services are missing. They struggle because the services aren’t coordinated. Several vendors mean several contracts, several points of contact and no single owner of the result.

We built Mandafia to fix that, with skilled people, clear service levels and honest reporting, delivered through one team.
